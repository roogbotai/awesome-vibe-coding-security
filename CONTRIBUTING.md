# Contributing

Thanks for helping make this list better. A few guidelines keep it useful.

## What belongs here

- Resources that are genuinely useful for securing apps built with AI tools (Lovable, Bolt, v0, Cursor, Replit) and their Supabase or Firebase backends.
- Guides, references, standards, and free or freemium tools that a non-expert builder can actually use.

## What to avoid

- Purely promotional links with no real value to the reader.
- Dead links, paywalled content presented as free, or duplicates of an existing entry.
- Anything you would not personally recommend to a friend shipping their first app.

## Format

Add your entry to the most relevant section, and follow this exact shape:

```
- [Name](https://example.com) - Short description that starts with a capital and ends with a period.
```

- Use HTTPS links wherever available.
- Keep the description to a single line.
- Put new entries in the section where a reader would look for them.

## How to submit

1. Fork this repository.
2. Add your entry in the right section.
3. Open a pull request describing what you added and why it is useful.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution is released under [CC0](LICENSE), the same terms as the rest of the list.
